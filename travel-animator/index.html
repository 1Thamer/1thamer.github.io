Animate your journey ✈️

Draw a multi-stop trip on a real world map, watch it animate with plane, car and boat models, then export the whole thing as a video — entirely inside your browser. No account, no API keys, nothing uploaded.

  • 1. Hit Draw route and click the map to drop stops — or pick a demo trip.
  • 2. Switch plane / car / boat before each click to set that leg's transport.
  • 3. Press Play, then Record to save a .webm video.

Help & shortcuts

How it works

Map tiles, place search and car routing come from free, keyless, CORS-open public services (Esri, OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, Photon/Nominatim, OSRM). The whole map is composited onto a single <canvas> so captureStream() can record it — that is why there is no Leaflet here.
